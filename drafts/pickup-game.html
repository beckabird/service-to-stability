Prepared for Nate Boyer

Nate, here's the game we'd like to build with you.

This is a working concept for the Rally Point Pickup Game. It's one Saturday at Fortera Stadium in Clarksville, where veterans, professional athletes, active duty service members and civilians play together. Veteran, active duty and civilian players are chosen by raffle from people who apply on the site. Every dollar raised opens Rally Point House: free, sober transitional housing for eight veterans near Fort Campbell.

The page below is what the public event page could look like. It isn't published anywhere. We built it so you could see the idea before we commit to any of it.

Already on board

Scott McEwen, author of American Sniper, has pledged signed books for the silent auction. He's also offered to join as a guest speaker if we need him, or to sign books on game day.

What we're hoping you'll consider:

  • 1
    Speak at the post-game huddle. Your topic, your length.
  • 2
    Help us run the day MVP-style: a shared workout before kickoff and honest conversation after.
  • 3
    Share the event page once it's live.

Everything else is open, including the date, the format and who's on the field. Tell us what would make it worth your time.

Saturday, May 15, 2027 · Clarksville, TennesseePreview

Rally Point Pickup Game.

Where veterans, professional athletes, active duty service members and civilians play together. Two mixed teams, one friendly pickup game. A community workout before kickoff and a huddle after, where veterans speak briefly about service, transition and peer support. Family friendly, with resource tables and a silent auction of donated sports memorabilia and signed books. Every dollar opens the door at Rally Point House.

Game day

Where, when and what to expect.

  • DateSaturday, May 15, 2027
  • Kickoff10:00 AM. Community workout and warm-up first.
  • WrapVeteran huddle and silent auction close by 2:00 PM.

Who's behind the game.

The cause

Rally Point House

Free, structured, sober transitional housing for eight veterans near Fort Campbell. Residents pay nothing and get a safe bed, a peer community and a path to work, benefits and a home of their own. The game's $25,000 goal is the startup fund that opens the door.

See the house

The nonprofit

The Service to Stability Project

A Clarksville, Tennessee nonprofit in formation, building veteran transitional living and support, from service to stability. Bed 1 is already covered by founding sponsor 4everbound.

About the project

The community

Foxhole Forum

Founded by Becka Karle and Christopher Sposti, MBA, CEI: U.S. Army infantry combat veteran, OEF Afghanistan, former Texas police officer, and Senior Veteran Behavioral Health Advisor at Patriot Power Up. Foxhole Forum brings veterans together for straight talk and peer support.

foxholevets.com

The model

Merging Vets and Players (MVP)

Founded in 2015 by Nate Boyer and Fox Sports NFL insider Jay Glazer. MVP pairs combat veterans with former pro athletes for weekly workouts and honest conversation, because both face the same hard question when the uniform comes off: what's next?

vetsandplayers.org
