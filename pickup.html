Saturday, May 15, 2027 · Clarksville, Tennessee

Rally Point Pickup Game.

Veterans and community players, two mixed teams, one friendly pickup game. A community workout before kickoff and a huddle after, where veterans speak briefly about service, transition and peer support. Family friendly, with resource tables and a silent auction of donated sports memorabilia. Every dollar opens the door at Rally Point House.

  • DateSaturday, May 15, 2027

Special guest speaker

Nate Boyer

U.S. Army Green Beret veteran. Former University of Texas walk-on and Seattle Seahawks long snapper. Cofounder, with Fox Sports' Jay Glazer, of Merging Vets and Players (MVP).

Nate speaks at the post-game huddle about service, transition and why no veteran should have to make the jump alone.

Who's behind the game.

The cause

Rally Point House

Free, structured, sober transitional housing for eight veterans near Fort Campbell. Residents pay nothing and get a safe bed, a peer community and a path to work, benefits and a home of their own. The game's $25,000 goal is the startup fund that opens the door.

See the house

The nonprofit

The Service to Stability Project

A Clarksville, Tennessee nonprofit in formation, building veteran transitional living and support, from service to stability. Bed 1 is already covered by founding sponsor 4everbound.

About the project

The community

Foxhole Forum

Founded by Becka Karle and Christopher Sposti, MBA, CEI: U.S. Army infantry combat veteran, OEF Afghanistan, former Texas police officer, and Senior Veteran Behavioral Health Advisor at Patriot Power Up. Foxhole Forum brings veterans together for straight talk and peer support.

foxholevets.com

The model

Merging Vets and Players (MVP)

Founded in 2015 by Nate Boyer and Fox Sports NFL insider Jay Glazer. MVP pairs combat veterans with former pro athletes for weekly workouts and honest conversation, because both face the same hard question when the uniform comes off: what's next?

vetsandplayers.org
