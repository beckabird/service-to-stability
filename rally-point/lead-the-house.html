Now recruiting

Lead the house. Live there free.

We're looking for a veteran in steady recovery to live at The Rally Point House and keep it running: the person residents check in with, learn from, and count on.

What you'll do

  • Live on site and be the house's day-to-day point person
  • Run the weekly house meeting; keep chores, curfew and house rules on track
  • Carry out drug testing under the house's written policy
  • Welcome new residents on move-in day and show them the ropes
  • Flag problems early to the Director of Programs; call 911 or 988 in an emergency
  • Keep simple daily logs: attendance, incidents, supplies

What you get

  • A private room in the house at no cost
  • Utilities and internet included
  • Training in house management, overdose response (naloxone) and de-escalation
  • Backup from our case manager and Director of Programs; you're not on your own
  • Leadership experience and a strong reference for your next step

Who we're looking for

Steady, fair, and calm under pressure.

  • A U.S. veteran, any branch, any discharge status (reviewed case by case)
  • If recovery is part of your story: at least one year of continuous sobriety
  • Leads by example and treats people fairly
  • Willing to complete a background check and sign a written House Manager agreement
  • Able to live on site in Clarksville, TN

House Manager is a live-in role defined by a written agreement. Terms are shared during the interview.
