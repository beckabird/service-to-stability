The house

Small on purpose. Steady by design.

Eight veterans, one shared home in Clarksville, a case manager and a live-in House Manager. Residents pay nothing.

  • WhereClarksville, Tennessee, near Fort Campbell, the VA clinic and bus lines. Location shared with residents and referral partners only.
  • Size8 beds in a shared 5-bedroom home, including our live-in House Manager
  • ResidentsMale veterans to start; a women's house is planned
  • StandardSober living, certified through a Tennessee state-approved recovery residence program
  • StaffCase manager plus a live-in veteran House Manager
  • StaySet by each resident's stability plan, typically 6 to 12 months

What residents pay

Nothing.

No rent, no fees, no deposit. Each week is sponsored by people in the community, so nobody is counting nights.

A sponsored week means a resident never pays for a night and never has to wonder if they'll be out tomorrow. They get seven days to settle in and work their plan.

Who it's for

Veterans between where they've been and where they're headed.

Eligible

  • Veterans of any branch, including Guard and Reserve
  • Any discharge status; other-than-honorable reviewed case by case
  • Committed to sobriety. Medication-assisted treatment welcome when following the prescribed plan
  • Ready to work a written stability plan

Common situations

  • Finishing residential treatment
  • Leaving a hospital or VA facility
  • In Veterans Treatment Court
  • Recently separated from the Army
  • At risk of homelessness

Bring if you have it: DD-214 or proof of service, photo ID, discharge or treatment summary, medication list. Missing paperwork isn't a barrier; help with records is part of intake.

What's included

Housing is the foundation. Support is the structure.

Housing

Safe, sober, furnished

A bed in a shared home, utilities and internet included.

Case management

One person in your corner

Weekly one-on-one time on your stability plan.

Benefits

VA and disability navigation

Help with VA health care, disability claims, SNAP and SSI/SSDI.

Employment

Work and income

Résumé help, job leads and veteran-friendly local employers.

Recovery

Continuing care

Connection to outpatient treatment, peer groups and VA mental health care.

Community

People who get it

Shared standards, shared meals, shared accountability.

House standards

Clear expectations keep the house safe for everyone.

  • Stay sober. Regular drug testing applies to every resident.
  • Attend the weekly house meeting and your case-management check-in.
  • Do your share of chores and keep common areas clean.
  • Keep curfew during your first 30 days while you settle in.
  • Work your plan: benefits, health care, work or school, and housing.
