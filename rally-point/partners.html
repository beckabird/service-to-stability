For referral partners

A clear handoff for the veterans you serve.

For VA social workers, treatment center discharge planners, Veterans Treatment Court staff and Fort Campbell transition teams.

Who we accept

  • Male veterans of any branch and discharge status (OTH reviewed case by case)
  • Committed to sobriety; MAT welcome when following the prescribed plan
  • Ready to work a written stability plan
  • Clarksville and Montgomery County area, or willing to relocate here

What we need from you

  • A referral through our form or a call to our intake director
  • The veteran's consent to be contacted
  • A discharge or move-out date if there is one
  • No medical or legal records through the website; we'll arrange that securely
