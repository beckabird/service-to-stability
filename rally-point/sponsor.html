Sponsor a week

$226 keeps a veteran in the house for a week.

Residents pay nothing. Each week of stay is sponsored by someone who decided a veteran in Clarksville shouldn't have to.

What your week covers

Everything the house provides.

  • Case management. Weekly one-on-one time to build and work the resident's stability plan: benefits, income, health care, recovery and housing.
  • Drug testing. Regular testing that keeps the house sober and safe for everyone in it.
  • House safety. Our live-in House Manager, house rules, supplies and the upkeep that keeps the house a safe place to land.
A sponsored week means a resident never pays for a night and never has to wonder if they'll be out tomorrow. They get seven days to settle in and work their plan.

Prefer a different amount? Any gift helps the house open: give on our donate page. Gifts go to The Service to Stability Project, the Tennessee nonprofit in formation that runs The Rally Point House. Whether gifts are tax-deductible depends on IRS approval of its 501(c)(3) status; your receipt will state the current status.

Why a week, not a night

Because nobody should be counting nights.

  • It gives residents a cushion. No pressure to leave after a night, and nobody pays one night at a time.
  • It's how the house runs. House meetings, case-management check-ins and job searches all happen on a weekly rhythm.
  • It puts your gift to work. A single night costs more to process than it's worth; a week is meaningful support.
