How it works

From first call to your own front door.

Five stages. The pace is yours; the direction is always forward.

  1. Day 1

    Referral

    A case manager, counselor, court, family member or the veteran reaches out.

  2. Week 1

    Intake screening

    Our intake director confirms fit and checks VA and benefit eligibility.

  3. Move-in

    Land

    A furnished bed, house expectations and a House Manager who shows you the ropes.

  4. Months 1 to 6

    Build

    A written stability plan for income, benefits, health care, recovery and housing.

  5. Graduation

    Move forward

    Into permanent housing, with follow-up support after move-out.

A typical week

Structure without a drill sergeant.

One house meeting, one case-management check-in, regular drug testing, chores, and the rest of the week spent on work, appointments, recovery meetings and the plan. Every week is sponsored, so the only thing a resident has to work on is moving forward.
