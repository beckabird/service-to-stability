Questions

What people ask first.

Straight answers about the house, who it's for and how it's paid for.

Are you open yet?

Not yet. Join the opening list and our intake director will reach out before we open.

Is it really free?

Yes. No rent, no fees, no deposit. Every week of a resident's stay is sponsored by the community.

Who can live there?

Male veterans of any branch and discharge status (other-than-honorable reviewed case by case) who are committed to sobriety and ready to work a stability plan.

Do I have to be sober?

Yes. It's a sober house with regular drug testing. Medication-assisted treatment is welcome when you follow your prescribed plan.

Can I keep my job, car or pet?

Jobs and cars, yes, and we encourage both. Pets and service animals are reviewed case by case.

Why do you ask sponsors for a week instead of a night?

A week gives residents stability, it's how the house runs, and a one-night gift costs more to process than it's worth. A sponsored week means nobody is counting nights.

Where does a $350 week go?

Case management, drug testing and house safety, including our live-in House Manager, supplies and upkeep, for one resident for one week.

Where is the house?

Clarksville, near Fort Campbell, the VA clinic and bus lines. To protect residents, the address is shared with residents and referral partners only.

What about women veterans?

A house for women veterans is part of the long-term plan.

Who runs the house?

The Service to Stability Project, a Tennessee nonprofit in formation. Learn more at project-pledge.com.

Need housing now?

Call the VA's National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838, any time. In a crisis, call 988 and press 1, or text 838255.
