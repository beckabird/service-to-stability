Why "rally point"

Where a unit regroups, accounts for everyone, and moves out together.

In the field, a rally point is the place you fall back to when things get scattered. You catch your breath, check your gear, make sure nobody's missing, and get a plan before you move.

That's what this house is for. Veterans leaving treatment, the hospital, the courts or the Army get a safe, sober place to land, people who get it, and a case manager who helps them plan the next move.

Sponsor a week

$350 keeps a veteran in the house for a week.

A week is long enough to settle in, make a plan and start working it. Every sponsored week covers:

  • Case management. Weekly one-on-one time to build and work the resident's stability plan: benefits, income, health care, recovery and housing.
  • Drug testing. Regular testing that keeps the house sober and safe for everyone in it.
  • House safety. Our live-in House Manager, house rules, supplies and the upkeep that keeps the house a safe place to land.
A sponsored week means a resident never pays for a night and never has to wonder if they'll be out tomorrow. They get seven days to settle in and work their plan.
