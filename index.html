Clarksville, Tennessee · Now raising our first house

A stable place to land. A strong path forward.

We're opening free, structured, sober housing for veterans in Clarksville, with a case manager who stays with each resident until they're set up for civilian life on their own terms.

Why Clarksville

Thousands of veterans come home to Clarksville. Not all of them have a place to land.

34,000

veterans live in Clarksville and Montgomery County, the second-most of any county in Tennessee.

≈4,500

soldiers leave the Army at Fort Campbell each year.

31%

of them stay in the area, well above the Army average of 24%.

Figures: City of Clarksville; Fort Campbell transition briefings (2020–2022). Counts are approximate.
